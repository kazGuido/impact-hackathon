# Hackathon brief (summary) – Shanghai Impact Hackathon, 21 Nov 2026

Track: Education and AI – "Help learners practise and demonstrate understanding with AI. A demo might show an attempt, a useful hint and a follow-up check. Explain how you handle AI limits and learner data."

## Submit by 17:30 (Google Form)
- Prototype: shareable link to a working demo (one core user journey). Check judges can open it.
- Pitch deck: max 3 main slides: user & problem, cited research, solution & demo evidence. Label assumptions.
- Build notes: what works, what uses sample data / simulated integrations, pre-existing work.
- Demo clip: 60-second screen recording of the core journey.
- Label sample data and simulated parts. No private data without permission.

## Shortlist (17:30–18:00, 8 of 12 teams advance)
Problem & user evidence 40% · Working core flow 40% · Learning impact 20%.

## Final judging (live pitch, 3–5 min)
- Learning problem & solution fit 35%
- Learning impact & evidence (how you know learning occurred) 25%
- Feasible & responsible product (AI limits, learner data) 20%
- Creativity & usability (supports learning, not just giving answers) 10%
- Team insight & pitch 10%
Coding method earns no extra points.

## Pitch pacing (playbook, 3 min)
Hook 0:00–0:20 · Pain 0:20–0:50 · Solution 0:50–1:20 · Outcome 1:20–1:50 · Proof 1:50–2:20 · Business & ask 2:20–3:00
